Reading the numbers honestly.
  • Elevation in exported GPX is often quantized to whole meters and draped over a smooth DEM, so raw gain under-counts; a densely-recorded device track with a small deadband over-counts. Same hill, different math.
  • GPX carries no surface, traffic or road-class data — "harder" here means physical effort only.
  • To compare routes rather than sources, re-drape both over one common DEM (a future toggle); for now, resample + smooth both identically and read a range, not a single figure.
